## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an elevated, editorial-grade portfolio presence tailored for design leadership and high-tier creative direction. It pairs structural restraint with atmospheric refinement, delivering high readability and understated luxury. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of modern minimalism and subtle tactile depth:
- **Clean Architectural Canvas:** Broad white space framed by cool off-white tones, giving featured projects structural breathing room.
- **Editorial Typographic Stance:** High-contrast typographic scales evoking independent design monographs and premium studio journals.
- **Subtle Chromatic Warmth:** Cool lavender micro-details, tinted borders, and deep slate navy anchors avoid the stark sterility of pure monochrome.
- **Perceived Exclusivity:** Quiet micro-interactions, low-saturation atmospheric shadows, and meticulously tuned spatial cadence.

## Colors

The palette balances clean cold-white architecture with the gravitas of deep purplish navy and refined mineral purples:

- **Canvas & Surface:**
  - `bg-primary` (`#F7F7FA`): Soft, cool off-white serving as the foundational viewport canvas.
  - `bg-surface` (`#FFFFFF`): Pure crisp white for elevated cards, active panels, and focal content surfaces.
  - `bg-surface-tinted` (`#F0EFF6`): Subtly muted lilac-neutral for nested wells, secondary metadata blocks, and tags.

- **Content & Typography:**
  - `text-primary` (`#252A4A`): Deep purplish navy delivering stark contrast, editorial authority, and softened optical weight compared to pure black.
  - `text-secondary` (`#5A5D7A`): Balanced mid-tone slate purple for body copy, project summaries, and subtitles.
  - `text-muted` (`#8B8CA3`): De-emphasized metadata, timestamps, and utility captions.

- **Brand & Accents:**
  - `brand-primary` (`#6256A5`): Royal amethyst purple used for dominant interactive states, active indicators, and prominent callouts.
  - `brand-accent` (`#A9A0D8`): Muted lilac for atmospheric glows, badge borders, hover transitions, and progress indicators.

- **Structural Outlines:**
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E0EC`): Delicate cool lavender-slate dividing lines, perimeter framing, and subtle separators.
  - `border-strong` (`#C4C1D9`): Focused input bounds and active card perimeters.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the sculptural modernity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings with the utilitarian precision of **Inter** for reading clarity:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set with negative tracking and generous line heights to establish clear editorial pacing across portfolio case studies.
- **Body & Captions (Inter):** Calibrated for long-form case studies, design documentation, and client testimonial blocks.
- **Metadata & Tags:** Small uppercase treatments in `label-sm` or `label-md` employ slight tracking expansion (`+0.04em` to `+0.05em`) to project architectural structure and catalog-like organization.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive 12-column dynamic grid system anchored by wide gutters and generous perimeter margins.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1360px`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`, `margin-desktop: 5rem`. Allows alternating asymmetric case study compositions, large visual spreads, and multi-column metadata sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin-tablet: 2.5rem`. Sidebars collapse beneath key imagery; grid pairs case cards two-up.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Case studies shift to single-column full-width stacks, prioritizing visual rhythm with condensed vertical spacing.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** High-volume vertical intervals between case modules (`space-xl` scaled to multiples of 48px–96px) evoke modern print editorial publications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies primarily on tonal separation and soft, ambient lilac-tinted shadows, preserving a pristine light environment without muddy gray drop-offs:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#F7F7FA` foundation with no shadow.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Static Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces sitting directly on the canvas, bounded by a 1px border of `#E2E0EC`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(37, 42, 74, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(98, 86, 165, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward projection. Shadow: `0 6px 20px -2px rgba(37, 42, 74, 0.05), 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(98, 86, 165, 0.08)`. Border shifts slightly toward `#C4C1D9`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Pinned Navigation):** Backdrop blur of `12px` with 80% opacity `#FFFFFF` fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), framed by `#E2E0EC` and an ambient glow shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(37, 42, 74, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (1)** shape language to project structural discipline and architectural rigor. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and chips use a controlled radius of `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`) to `0.375rem`.
- Showcase cards, image viewports, and modals scale up to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) and `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- High-curvature or full pill shapes are avoided across standard containers to maintain a serious, high-end editorial feel; pill geometry is reserved strictly for specialized status indicators and micro-badges.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#6256A5` fill with pure white text, 1px border in `#6256A5`. On hover, transitions to a deeper `#51458E` with a faint lilac edge glow.
  - *Secondary:* Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `#252A4A` text and `#E2E0EC` outline. Hover shifts background to `#F0EFF6` with border darkening to `#C4C1D9`.
  - *Text / Ghost:* Unpadded or inline variant in `#6256A5` with an understated directional micro-arrow animation on hover.

- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Built with `#F0EFF6` background, 1px border in `#E2E0EC`, and `#252A4A` text (`label-sm`).
  - Active/Selected state: `#FFFFFF` background, `#6256A5` text, and `#A9A0D8` border.

- **Cards (Case Studies):**
  - Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px `#E2E0EC` border.
  - Generous internal padding (`space-lg`), containing rich responsive media with subtle zoom transitions (1.02x transform) on parent card hover.

- **Form Fields & Inputs:**
  - `#FFFFFF` background, `#252A4A` text, and `#E2E0EC` default border.
  - Focus state: Crisp 1.5px border in `#6256A5` with a delicate, non-intrusive focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(169, 160, 216, 0.25)`.
  - Helper and error labels adhere to `body-sm` in `#8B8CA3` or designated crimson accents.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#C4C1D9` border.
  - Checked: Solid `#6256A5` fill with a pure white check/dot glyph.

- **Portfolio-Specific Components:**
  - *Case Study Index Item:* Full-width horizontal list row separated by hairline `#E2E0EC` dividers. Reveals thumbnail preview and transitions title color to `#6256A5` on row hover.
  - *Sticky Header Bar:* Floating or docked navigation bar with frosted translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` + 16px blur) bounded by a bottom `#E2E0EC` rule.
